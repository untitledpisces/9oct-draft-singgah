About

A place to rest,
to belong.

Singgah Sana is a private estate in the hills of Kampung Janda Baik. It was built slowly, by hand, with the belief that rest is not a luxury but a practice — and that a home can belong to a landscape without interrupting it.

Our Story

Built slowly,
with the land.

Singgah Sana began as a family plot at the edge of the Titiwangsa range — a slope of old rubber trees and river stone, looking out toward the Bentong hills. The first pavilion was raised in 2019, by a small team of local carpenters working entirely by hand.

Every material was chosen for how it would age. Reclaimed chengal timber from old kampung houses. Atap roofs that would weather with the rain. River stone laid by craftsmen who had known this valley their whole lives.

The result is not a resort — it is a home, kept open. Four acres of gardens, a small pond, five bedrooms across four pavilions. Reserved for one party at a time, so that the estate stays quiet, and belongs entirely to whoever is here.

What We Believe

The principles
behind the place.

Everything at Singgah Sana comes back to a small set of ideas — about slowness, craft, and what it means to host well.

01

Slowness, on purpose

Nothing here is designed to hurry you. No schedules, no queues, no noise after dark. The estate moves at the pace of the light and the weather.

02

Craft, where it lasts

Timber posts, atap roofs, river stone walls. Built by hand, from materials that will only grow more beautiful with time.

03

The land first

The gardens, the pond, the forest at the back of the estate — we plan around them, not over them. Wildlife has right of way.

04

One party at a time

The whole estate is reserved for a single booking. No other guests, no shared facilities, no interruptions.

05

Hosting, not service

Our team is small and present — quietly attentive, never hovering. Breakfast when you wake, help when you ask.

06

Local by default

Produce from the valley, staff from the kampung, guides from the hills around us. The estate belongs to this place.
